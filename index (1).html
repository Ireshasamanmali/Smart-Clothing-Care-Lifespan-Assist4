Natural vs Synthetic Care

Natural fabrics like Cotton and Silk absorb high ambient humidity in Sri Lanka. Always ensure silk is washed in cold water and air-dried flat in shaded, well-ventilated areas.

🌧️ Care Strategy: Monsoon / Continuous Heavy Rain

Drying Recommendation: Use indoor drying racks combined with an electric fan or dehumidifier. Apply an extra spin cycle in your washing machine to remove up to 30% more residual water.

Storage Strategy: Maintain 2 to 3 inches of space between closet hangers. Use breathable cotton garment covers instead of plastic bags.

🛡️ Mold & Musty Odor Prevention: Ensure thick garment seams and pockets are 100% dry before putting away. Fungal spores proliferate rapidly above 75% relative humidity.

🛠️ Quick Maintenance Tips

  • Tea / Curry Stains: Rinse immediately with cold water. Apply liquid detergent and lemon juice before washing.
  • Loose Buttons: Use double-strand cotton thread with cross-stitch securing to prevent thread slip.
  • Hard Water Buildup: Add 1/2 cup of distilled white vinegar to the final rinse cycle to dissolve calcium mineral scaling.

📚 Educational Clothing Care Guide

Master ISO 3758 laundry care symbols, prevent common mistakes, and extend your wardrobe's lifespan.

5 Core ISO 3758 Symbols

Learn to decode the standard 5 care symbols: Washtub (Washing), Triangle (Bleaching), Square (Drying), Iron (Ironing), and Circle (Professional Care).

11 Common Laundry Errors

Avoid overloading washing machines, using excessive detergent powder, mixing dark and light fabrics, and wringing delicate silk or wool fibers.

Sustainable Garment Lifespan

Washing clothes inside out in cold water reduces energy consumption by 75%, preserves dye molecules, and extends clothing life by up to 3 years.

Project Mission & Impact

The Smart Clothing Care & Lifespan Assistant is an AI-powered initiative created to address premature garment degradation caused by Sri Lanka's high ambient humidity, intense tropical sunlight, and monsoonal rainfall.
